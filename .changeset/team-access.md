Invite your team. The dashboard has a Team page where owners and admins create single-use invite links (7 days, optionally locked to one email), set roles (owner, admin, member) and remove people, who are signed out at once. Team logins are stored in your own database as scrypt hashes; invite tokens only as SHA-256 hashes. The ingest function gains /admin/team routes, and the stores gain member and invite methods. Settings changes now need an owner or admin, and sign-in is rate-limited.
